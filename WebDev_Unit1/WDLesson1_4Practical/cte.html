Computer Programming and Web Design

In Bayside High School, the "Computer Programminhg and Web Design" CTE allows real world exposure to the art of programming. You will learn to code in languages such as Python, JavaScript, Java, and more to build an education used in the workplace. By the end of high school, you will have a foundational knowledge of web development, general coding, and computer science. All of such are very practical uses for the real world that can build your future career early.
